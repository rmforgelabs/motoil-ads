Questions

Short answers to what people ask us most.

What do you build?

Android apps and mobile games. Apps are built with Flutter and Firebase, and games are built in Unity.

Can you take on my project?

Yes. We are open to new work of any size, from a small fix to a full app or game. Send us a short email about the idea.

What if the requirements change?

That is normal, and we welcome it. We talk through what the change means, then adjust the plan together.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. We fix bugs, add features, and keep builds working with new Android versions.

How do we get started?

Email rmforgelabs@gmail.com with a few lines about what you want to build. We will reply and take it from there.

Have an idea? Let's build it.

Tell us a few lines about your app or game, a rough timeline if you have one, and any challenges you already know about. We welcome all of it.
